Features

Everything you need for clean data

Powerful automation built specifically for lead lists, customer databases and contact sheets.

🧹
Smart Auto-Fix

Converts names, states and cities to Title Case, and cleans +91, 0 prefixes, spaces and dashes from phone numbers.

⚠️
Deduplication Engine

Finds exact duplicates across your key columns, keeps the first entry and moves the rest into a separate report.

📱
Phone Validation

Detects numbers that are not 10 digits and separates them, so you can send them back to your sourcing team.

📊
Cross-File Comparison

Upload two sheets and instantly find matching records between them. Ideal for checking new leads against old ones.

❤️
Data Health Score

See a clear percentage score showing how much of your file was clean, plus counts of blanks, duplicates and invalid rows.

🔒
Private & Secure

Processed files are stored only for you and are automatically deleted after 24 hours.
